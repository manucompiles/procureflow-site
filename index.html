What it is

A control tower on top of what you already use.

Not an ERP. Nothing to migrate to. Nothing new to learn.

Your stock stays in your Excel. Your orders stay yours. Your people keep working exactly as today. ProcureFlow sits on top: it reads, watches, projects every item forward, and speaks up before trouble, on the phone you already carry.

We don't replace your tools. We give them a brain and a voice.

How it works

8:00 AM, every day, without being asked.

Told as a clock, because that is how it runs.

08:00
It reads your sheet and simulates every item's future: steady sales going out, committed orders due, trucks coming in. Every item, every morning, the way your best buyer would if they had the time.
08:01
"HR Coil breaks its buffer in 9 days. Buy 640 MT from SAIL, cheapest that still lands in time, sized for Verma's order too. Place by Thursday." One tap: approve. Every recommendation shows its arithmetic; you can argue with it, and it answers.
Then
It follows through. It hands the order to your buyer with the deadline, writes the PO row into your sheet the moment they place it, chases the late truck, and tells you delivered, loop closed. When a mill refuses or quotes late, it compares the others on the spot and lays out the real choice: switch, wait, or neither, with which customer waits and for how long.
Control

It never spends a rupee without you.

Control is the feature, not a limitation.

  • Every order waits for a human tap. No exceptions, ever, by design.
  • Autonomy is earned and rationed: when you trust it on routine repeat buys, you set limits per item, and it acts only inside them.
  • You decide the important 20%. It stops needing you for the boring 80%.
  • Every decision stays in the chat as a record: what was proposed, what you chose, what it cost.
The compounding

Every order you run teaches it your business.

Month 6 beats month 1. Year 2 beats year 1.

  • It learns your real sales rates: measurements, not estimates.
  • It learns which mills actually deliver on time, and plans around each one's real behaviour.
  • It remembers what only the owner knows today: "festival week the trucks jam", "that supplier slips in monsoon". Told once, applied forever.
  • It becomes your most experienced employee, and it never resigns.
